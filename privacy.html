Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

This policy covers the website at this address and The Peak Second Publisher (the "App"), a private tool the channel owner uses to publish The Peak Second's own videos to its own social media accounts.

Who uses the App

Only the owner of The Peak Second uses the App, and only to manage The Peak Second's own accounts. The App does not offer sign-up to the public and does not access other people's accounts.

What data the App accesses

  • Account authorization: when the owner connects a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, the platform issues an access token. The App uses it only to upload videos, set titles, captions and privacy settings, and read the status of those uploads.
  • Basic profile information of the connected account (account name, ID and avatar), used only to show which account a post goes to.
  • The channel's own content: the video files, titles, descriptions and captions that the owner chooses to publish.

The App does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other users, and it does not read private messages, comments or analytics of other accounts.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens and content are stored only on the owner's own computer or with the publishing service the owner uses to send uploads. Data is sent only to the platform the video is being published to (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta/Instagram). We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using features that publish to YouTube, the account owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

TikTok and Instagram

Publishing to TikTok is subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy, and publishing to Instagram to the Meta Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked in each app's settings under connected or authorized apps.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account stays connected and are deleted when the account is disconnected. To request deletion of any data the App holds, email thepeaksecond@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
